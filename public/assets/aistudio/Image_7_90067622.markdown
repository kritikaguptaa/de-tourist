## Brand & Style

This design system pairs the tactile authenticity of archival travel journals with the precise ergonomic demands of a high-performance desktop web app. The aesthetic balances physical nostalgia—creamy heavy stock paper, gentle ticket perforations, postal stamps, and polaroid proportions—against razor-sharp typographic hierarchy and immediate digital utility.

### Emotional Disposition
- **Curated & Mindful:** Slow travel values embedded directly into interface pacing, avoiding high-urgency badges or gamified noise.
- **Physicality & Texture:** Evokes archival ephemera, hand-stamped documents, and curated guidebooks without compromising desktop speed and accessibility.
- **Precision Utility:** Clean, unambiguous information density for routes, schedules, and itinerary logs.

### Aesthetic Methodology
The aesthetic draws on warm tactile minimalism mixed with editorial cartography. Visual noise is minimized by trading saturated gradients and glowing blur effects for layered off-white paper stocks, subtle grain-tinted borders, and intentional hairline rules.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in sunbaked mineral pigments and natural paper fibers, steering clear of stark digital blues or ungrounded synthetic hues.

### Primary & Day Indicators
- **Primary / Terracotta (`#E26D4F`):** Core interactive focus, primary actions, callouts, and Day 1 itinerary markers.
- **Secondary / Sage (`#8FB39A`):** Ecological notes, nature-focused landmarks, and Day 2 itinerary routes.
- **Tertiary / Sky (`#6BA6CD`):** Waterways, transit links, and Day 3 itinerary events.
- **Accent / Sun (`#F2B84B`):** Golden hour alerts, highlighted moments, and leisure indicators.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Base Canvas (`#F6F1E7`):** Warm cream foundation resembling untreated book paper.
- **Card & Canvas Highlight (`#FFFFFF`):** Bleached stationery white for elevated cards and polaroid frames.
- **Surface Muted (`#FAF7F2`):** Low-contrast grouped containers.
- **Border / Edge (`#E6DFD3`):** Warm deckled edges and card delineators.
- **Ink Primary (`#111827`):** Deep carbon ink for headline precision and high legibility.
- **Ink Secondary (`#1F2A37`):** Slightly softened text for metadata, captions, and body copy.

## Typography

The typographical structure creates an immediate tension between the human literary cadence of travel memoirs and the rigid telemetry of transit timetables.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** High-contrast, editorial elegance used exclusively for destination names, chapter headers, and storytelling narrative blocks.
- **Interface & Content Body (DM Sans):** Neutral, open geometric sans-serif that ensures rapid readability across dense travel logs, forms, and desktop control panels.
- **Telemetry & Schedule Data (Space Mono):** Used for departure times, coordinates, buffer windows, ticket numbers, and cost breakdowns to evoke physical stamps, receipts, and boarding passes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a desktop-first fluid structure bounded within maximum readable constraints, reminiscent of broadsheet magazines or open atlas layouts.

- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column responsive layout with fixed proportions on desktop (`max-w-7xl`), splitting cleanly between an interactive cartographic/scrapbook canvas and an itinerary timeline.
- **Rhythm:** Generous margins simulate the empty matte borders of art albums. Elements maintain structured vertical gaps using the 8px baseline (`space-xs` through `space-xl`).
- **Reflow & Responsiveness:** Desktop side-by-side splits (Map & Feed) collapse gracefully into a stacked layout on viewports narrower than 1024px, maintaining consistent edge padding through adaptive margin tokens.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than synthetic multi-color glow or high-blur drop shadows, visual depth is achieved through physical paper stratification and crisp, directional cast shadows.

- **Paper Layering:** Surfaces build depth from the deep canvas (`#F6F1E7`) to mid-level panels (`#FAF7F2`) and foreground white cards (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Shadow Signature:** Shadows feature a distinct warm tint and minimal diffusion:
  - *Resting card:* `0 2px 4px rgba(31, 42, 55, 0.04), 0 8px 16px rgba(226, 109, 79, 0.03)`
  - *Hovered/Lifted card:* `0 6px 12px rgba(31, 42, 55, 0.07), 0 16px 24px rgba(31, 42, 55, 0.05)`
  - *Stamp/Pin elements:* Crisp `0 1px 2px rgba(31, 42, 55, 0.12)` offset mimicking inked relief.
- **Structural Outlines:** Every card and modal is enclosed in a hairline `1px solid #E6DFD3` stroke, maintaining boundary clarity against tone-on-tone ivory backdrops.

## Shapes

The shape system favors restrained, tailored softness (`0.25rem` base, `0.5rem` on cards) echoing precision-trimmed stationery paper, rather than overly bulbous or bubble-like silhouettes.

- **Cards & Modals (`rounded-lg` / 0.5rem):** Subtle paper corner rounding that avoids harsh razor edges while remaining crisp and structured.
- **Polaroid Framing:** Generous asymmetric bottom padding (`space-xl`) inside cards, paired with compact horizontal and top margins, reproducing classic photo borders.
- **Perforated Edges:** Applied to boarding-pass cards and day dividers using small radial mask cutouts (`mask-image: radial-gradient`) or subtle dashed border dividers with a warm ink hue.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Terracotta background (`#E26D4F`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded` (0.25rem), with an inner subtle paper-press stroke. Hover state transitions to a deepened clay tone (`#CF5A3D`).
- **Secondary:** Off-white card surface (`#FFFFFF`), ink text (`#1F2A37`), framed in a `1px` border (`#E6DFD3`).
- **Ghost / Utility:** Transparent background with subtle terracotta text and under-hover highlight tint (`rgba(226, 109, 79, 0.08)`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with warm cream border (`#E6DFD3`). Focus activates a 1px solid terracotta border with a 2px diffuse terracotta halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Monospace-accented selection boxes with an ink checkmark; checked state fills with Terracotta (`#E26D4F`).

### Cards & Polaroid Wrappers
- **Scrapbook Cards:** Pure white core, bordered by `#E6DFD3`, layered with faint drop-shadow.
- **Media Containers:** Image containers replicate polaroid prints with slightly desaturated tone mappings and uncropped aspects framed by an asymmetric bottom white lip.

### Chips & Day Badges
- **Day Markers:** Compact pill tags featuring `ticket-data` typography.
  - Day 1: Terracotta background wash with solid `#E26D4F` text.
  - Day 2: Sage wash with solid `#8FB39A` text.
  - Day 3: Sky wash with solid `#6BA6CD` text.
- **Category Chips:** Minimal outlines with uppercase monospace sizing and small circular ink bullets.

### Transit & Buffer Indicators
- **Buffer Gauges:** Horizontal connecting rules between nodes rendered as dashed tracks (`#E6DFD3`) interrupted by a centered Space Mono ticket badge denoting rest windows (e.g., `+45M CALM BUFFER`).
